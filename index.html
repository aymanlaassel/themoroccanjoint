From Morocco to Miami

Real Moroccan cooking, more than 20 years in the making

Our chef came to Miami carrying the flavors she grew up with in Morocco: spices warming in the kitchen, bread shared around the table, tajines left to cook slowly until everything becomes tender and full of flavor.

After more than 20 years of cooking, those memories still guide every dish. Preserved lemon, olives, cumin, saffron, fresh herbs and mint tea are not simply ingredients. They are part of the food she knows, loves and wants to share.

The Moroccan Joint is her way of bringing that feeling of home to Miami: honest food, generous portions and recipes made with love.

Preserved lemonSalt-cured for weeks, then sliced into the chicken tajine.

Cool down with Ice Atay

Moroccan mint tea, reimagined for Miami

Moroccan mint tea is traditionally served hot and poured with care. Our chef created Ice Atay as a refreshing Miami twist on that beloved tradition: fragrant mint tea chilled over ice with a lemonade twist, bright, smooth and perfect beside a warm panini or a slow-cooked tajine.
